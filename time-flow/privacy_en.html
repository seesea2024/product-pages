Privacy Policy

Overview

Welcome to TimeAssistant! We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

TimeAssistant is designed to store most of your data locally on your device. The information we collect includes:

  • Time Record Data: Time records you create, activity descriptions, tags, and other related information
  • Health Data: Blood pressure, pulse, and other health information you choose to record
  • Device Information: Basic device information used to improve app performance
  • iCloud Data: If you enable iCloud sync, data is stored in your iCloud account

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain core app functionality
  • Improve user experience and app performance
  • Sync data across your devices via iCloud
  • Respond to user support requests

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: All your data is stored locally on your device by default, using Apple's secure encryption mechanisms.

iCloud Sync: If you enable iCloud sync, data is transmitted and stored via Apple's iCloud service, protected by Apple's security mechanisms.

We Don't Collect: We do not send your data to our own servers, nor do we sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Permission Explanations

  • Camera Permission: Used to capture photos of blood pressure monitors and other devices for OCR recognition (only requested when you use this feature)
  • Microphone Permission: Used for voice input functionality (only requested when you use this feature)
  • Speech Recognition Permission: Used to parse your voice input (only requested when you use this feature)
  • Photos Permission: Used to select device photos for OCR recognition (only requested when you use this feature)
  • Siri Permission: Used to control timer and time tracking via Siri (only requested when you use this feature)

Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • Access all your data
  • Export your data (PDF and CSV formats)
  • Delete all or part of your data
  • Disable iCloud sync at any time

Children's Privacy

Our app is not specifically intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will notify you within the app and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of the page.
