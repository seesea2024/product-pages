Privacy Policy

TimeFlow · Privacy

How we collect, use, store, and protect your data.

1. About the app

TimeFlow (TimeAssistant) is a focused time tracking and health logging app for iOS and macOS. Your data lives on your device by default and only syncs through your own iCloud account when you turn sync on.

Core features

  • Time logging: Timeline view, time interval & time point records, custom tags and filters.
  • Timer: Built-in timer with preset activity templates, start and stop in one tap.
  • Analytics: Beautiful breakdowns across day, week, month, and year.
  • Health data: On-device OCR for blood pressure readings; manual entry for weight and more.
  • Export: Export records as PDF for sharing and archiving.
  • iCloud sync: Optional CloudKit sync, can be disabled at any time.
  • Voice & Siri: System dictation and Siri shortcuts for hands-free control.

2. Data we collect

We follow a strict data-minimisation policy:

  • We do not collect personal information, and we never upload your time logs or health data to third-party servers.
  • No account is required. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics SDK.
  • When iCloud sync is enabled, your records live inside the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID. The developer has no read access to user-private data.

Our commitment: your time logs, tags, and health data always belong to you.

3. Data storage

3.1 Local storage

All records are stored in the iOS / macOS app sandbox via SwiftData and Core Data. We do not rely on any external database or third-party cloud service.

3.2 iCloud sync (optional)

When you enable iCloud sync in Settings, time slices and tags are synced through CloudKit into your Apple ID's private database:

  • Container identifier: iCloud.com.stone.TimeFlow
  • The developer can only write data; we cannot read your private database.
  • Disabling sync keeps your local data; previously synced data is managed by you in iCloud Settings.

4. Device permissions

Permissions are requested only when the corresponding feature is used:

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used to capture blood pressure monitor photos for OCR. Images are processed on-device and never uploaded.
  • Photos (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used to pick an existing photo for OCR. Selected images are only used for the current recognition and are not uploaded.
  • Microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription): Used by the system dictation framework. The app does not store raw audio.
  • Speech recognition (NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription): Used to transcribe dictation. Only text results are returned and stored.
  • Siri (NSSiriUsageDescription): Used to respond to Siri shortcuts that control the timer.
  • Remote notifications (aps-environment): Used for silent CloudKit sync pushes. We do not send marketing notifications.

You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy.

5. OCR and image processing

  • Blood pressure OCR is performed entirely on-device. No image is ever sent to a server.
  • Recognised values (structured data + original text) are saved with the record on-device and in your iCloud if sync is on.
  • You can delete any record or all data at any time.

6. In-app purchases

  • The app offers a Premium membership (com.stone.TimeFlow.premium) processed via Apple StoreKit 2.
  • Payments and receipts are handled by Apple. The app never sees your card details.
  • Free users get 30 uses of premium features. The counter is stored only on-device.

7. Third-party services

Beyond Apple's own system services (StoreKit, CloudKit, Siri, Speech), the app does not include any third-party SDK, analytics, advertising, or social components.

8. Children's privacy

The app is intended for general audiences. It is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Export and deletion

  • Export: Records can be exported as PDF.
  • Delete a record: Available from any record's detail view.
  • Delete everything: Uninstalling the app removes all local data. If iCloud sync is on, also delete the app's data in iCloud Settings.
