1. What TimeFlow can do

TimeFlow is built around a small set of features that work really well:

  • Timeline view: Day, week, month, and year views for all your records.
  • Tags: Custom tags like #work, #study, #exercise, with quick filtering.
  • Timer: One-tap tracking with preset activity templates.
  • Analytics: Donut charts and breakdowns by day, week, month, and year.
  • Blood pressure OCR: Snap a photo of a blood pressure monitor and the values are read on-device.
  • Weight log: Template-driven entry for weight and notes.
  • PDF export: Export records as PDF for sharing or archiving.
  • iCloud sync: Optional CloudKit sync across your devices.
  • Voice & Siri: System dictation plus Siri shortcuts for hands-free control.

2. Quick start

2.1 Create your first record

  1. Open TimeFlow — the Timeline tab is the default.
  2. Tap the + button in the top-right and choose a time interval or time point.
  3. Fill in the start / end time, an optional activity name, and any tags.
  4. Tap Create. The record appears on the timeline.

2.2 Use the timer

  1. Switch to the Timer tab.
  2. Pick a preset activity, or open Custom to enter a new name and tags.
  3. Tap Start to begin tracking.
  4. Tap Stop when done — TimeFlow creates a time-interval record for you.

2.3 Log blood pressure with OCR

  1. In the Timeline tab, tap + and choose the Blood pressure template.
  2. Tap the camera icon and point at your monitor.
  3. TimeFlow processes the image on-device and auto-fills systolic, diastolic, and pulse.
  4. Review the values and save.

2.4 Enable iCloud sync

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Turn on iCloud Sync (uses one free premium use, or requires Premium).
  3. Wait a few seconds — the status dot turns green once sync is ready.

3. Premium

Free users get 30 uses of premium features. After that you can unlock Premium to get:

  • iCloud sync: automatic cross-device backup.
  • OCR: unlimited blood pressure OCR.
  • PDF export: unlimited exports.

Open Settings → Premium to see remaining free uses, purchase, or restore.

4. Frequently asked questions

Q: Does TimeFlow collect my data?

A: No. Records live on-device by default. If you turn on iCloud sync, they go into your own Apple ID's private CloudKit database. The developer cannot read your data.

Q: Why does the app need Camera / Photos access?

A: To recognise blood pressure monitor screens. The permission is requested only when you actually use OCR. Images are processed on-device and never uploaded.

Q: Why does the app need Microphone / Speech recognition?

A: To support system dictation so you can create records by voice. Audio is handled by the system frameworks; TimeFlow does not store raw audio.

Q: How do I turn off iCloud sync?

A: Open Settings → iCloud Sync and toggle it off. Your local records remain. Anything previously synced is managed by you in iCloud Settings.

Q: What happens when I run out of free premium uses?

A: Premium-only features (iCloud sync, OCR, PDF export) are locked, but core features — time tracking, tags, timer, and analytics — keep working.

Q: How do I export my data?

A: From a record's detail view or from Settings, choose Export PDF. TimeFlow generates a PDF covering the selected date range.

Q: Can I delete a single record or all my data?

A: Yes. Tap a record to open its detail view, then tap Delete. To clear everything, uninstall the app.

Q: Is there a macOS version?

A: Yes. TimeFlow ships for iOS and macOS, with a menu bar quick entry on the Mac.
