Privacy

Your time data stays yours.

TimeFlow is local-first and only syncs to your own iCloud when you ask it to. No accounts, no ads, no third-party trackers.

  • Local first: Records live on your device by default. The app works fully offline.
  • Opt-in iCloud sync: Only when you turn it on, and only into your private CloudKit database.
  • On-device OCR: Blood pressure photos are processed locally and never uploaded.
  • No accounts, no tracking: No signups, no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • You're in control: Export to PDF, toggle iCloud, delete data, or uninstall at any time.
